FIP-0118 · Solstice

Reward split timeline & quarterly gate cycle

Every block reward splits three ways: consensus (w1) to miners, service (w2) to Orchestrators, and burn (w0), which is destroyed. Over nine quarters w1 ramps down on a fixed schedule while w2 steps up only when a quarter’s settlement volume clears its gate — whatever those two leave over is burned. Flip any gate to see the consequence. Every date derives from the activation date; FIP-0118 names none.

Assumptions & caveats

Planning assumptions, not protocol
  1. The activation date is an assumption. October 15, 2026 is a planning placeholder; FIP-0118 names no specific activation date or epoch. Change it above and every date on this page moves with it.
  2. Quarter length is ~90 days here. Actual length is set by EPOCHS_PER_QUARTER, so real quarter boundaries will drift from these dates.
  3. The 3-day posting period, 7-day verification window and 7-day SWA_TIMELOCK are proposed governance-repo values, not parameters of the FIP. Of the three, only SWA_TIMELOCK is enforced at L1.
  4. The Community Report deadline (7 days after quarter close) comes from governance repo doc 04 and overlaps the verification window.
  5. PR #1279 (transparency rules) was in Draft as of August 2026. The genesis disclosure and Community Report obligations shown here depend on it merging.
  6. Ordering within the post-verification window is indicative. Events after QE+10d (dashed outlines) are drawn in dependency order; the FIP sets no per-step deadline between QE+10d and the timelock expiry.
  7. w2 steps are drawn at the quarter boundary. In practice a passing gate is written to f02 after QE+10d and only takes effect when SWA_TIMELOCK expires, roughly QE+17d.
